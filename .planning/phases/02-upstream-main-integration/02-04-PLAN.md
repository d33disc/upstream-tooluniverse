<objective>
Turn the re-merge stage into an accountable finding set. Diff the whole re-derived tree against what `f81448f2` actually produced (D-07), classify every disagreement, apply D-06a's self-heal recheck against the pinned tree, then join the result to all 1,392 entries of Phase 1's `preservation.json` so criterion 3 -- "account for every custom code, tool, plugin, registration, and symlink asset" -- becomes a completeness assertion rather than a judgment call.

Purpose: the phase's central output. A disagreement is not automatically a defect; upstream superseding a shared definition is the expected correct outcome under D-08. What must never happen is a fork-only asset silently disappearing with nobody able to say so.

Output: `findings.json` (classified disagreements), `preservation-reclass.json` (1,392 dispositions), and `02-FINDINGS.md` (the human-readable review surface).
</objective>
